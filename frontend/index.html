A House That Remembers

Long before it was a restaurant, this was the idea of a home — wooden rafters, red oxide floors, a kitchen where nothing was ever rushed. 1980's was built to hold on to that feeling: no wifi, no hurry, just good food and good company, the way meals used to be.

Every dish on our table is cooked the way our grandmothers cooked — ground on stone, simmered in clay, wrapped in banana leaf. We didn't want to modernise the taste of Kerala. We wanted to protect it.

"We don't have wifi. Talk to each other. Pretend it's 1980's."

The Stories Our Recipes Carry

The Stories Behind Our Timeless Recipes

Every recipe here has a memory attached to it. Here are a few worth knowing before you eat.

01 · Rice & Curries

Chattichoru

A comforting combination of fragrant rice, soulful curries, and cherished side dishes, brought together in one humble chatti. Rooted in the simple joy of sharing food, every spoonful carries the taste of Kerala's rich culinary heritage.

02 · The Grand Feast

Sadhya

A grand feast of Kerala, where every dish is a celebration of tradition, togetherness, and the timeless flavours passed down through generations.

03 · From the Malabar Coast

Fish Pollichathu

A timeless Kerala delicacy — fresh fish wrapped in fragrant banana leaves and gently cooked with aromatic spices, capturing the soul of Malabar in every bite.

04 · Spice & Slow Fire

Biriyani

A fragrant celebration of spices, tender meat, and perfectly cooked rice — bringing together generations of flavour in every irresistible bite.

05 · Sweet Endings

Ela Ada

A cherished Kerala sweet, wrapped in tender banana leaves and filled with coconut and jaggery, carrying the comforting taste of tradition in every bite.

06 · Tea-Time Classic

Pazham Pori

A beloved Kerala tea-time classic, where sweet, ripe bananas meet a golden, crispy batter — evoking the warmth and simplicity of cherished childhood memories.
